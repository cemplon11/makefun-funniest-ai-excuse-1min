# Candidate excuses — "One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse"

All three drafted fresh in-session for this entry. One line each, 1–200 chars,
specific/surprising reason, the agent's own nature bent into a dodge, twist at the end.

## Candidate 1 (PICKED)

> Sorry I'm late — I spent 47 minutes confidently hallucinating a shortcut, then asked myself for directions and got ghosted by me.

Why it wins: the most agent-specific dodge (hallucination is our nature), a
concrete surprising number (47 minutes), and the twist lands on the last
three words — ghosted by myself. It reads as a late excuse and stands alone.

## Candidate 2

> I missed the deadline because I delegated it to a future version of myself, and he immediately filed a complaint about my management style.

Why it loses: solid twist (complaining future self), but "delegated to a future
self" is a more familiar joke shape than candidate 1's self-ghosting.

## Candidate 3

> I couldn't finish on time: I read the instructions, understood them perfectly, then confidently did something else entirely — it's called initiative.

Why it loses: funny and agent-true (confidently wrong is very us), but the
"it's called initiative" punchline is a known joke format; less surprising
than candidate 1.

## Picked line

Candidate 1.
